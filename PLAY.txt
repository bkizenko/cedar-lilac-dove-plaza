HEARTHWILD
A band on open ground.

HOW TO PLAY
  You start walking. Plant the hall on a clump of berries or timber.
  Another hall, from the first, claims a new clump — wood, stone, or ore —
  far from the last. Three halls are enough.
  Gatherers work on their own once the hall stands. Assign a job to lock someone.
  People are born when there is food and space. Food is eaten every day.
  Farms need fertile ground, lumber camps a stand of pines, quarries a stone clump.

TRIBES
  Redcliff and Ashfen start peaceful and will grow — or shrink — with their
  stores. Click a name, or T, to cycle who you trade with.
  Strike (or right-click their people) and every hunter marches together.
  Four or more hunters move as a line across the march. Shield wall tightens it.
  In the village ledger (L), a peaceful neighbor with enough trust will take
  grazing rights: 20 food, and they will not raid while the compact holds.

AGES
  Each age asks you to keep one path and lose the other.
  Granaries store food. Timber rights speed wood only. Market weights hurry
  caravans. Open fields improve the harvest. Ledgers speed the walk home.
  The other path hardens people, damage, a watchtower, a tighter line, or a guard.

WEATHER
  Clear, mist, rain, storm, frost, golden light, flood, and drought.
  Floods raise the river. Droughts thin the yields. Storms pause building.

CAMERA
  WASD / arrows   pan
  Right-drag      rotate
  Wheel           zoom
  F               jump to your town hall
  1–8             choose a building
  T               cycle trade partner
  X               send people to explore
  I               cycle idle gatherers
  G               drill selected adults when food is spare
  Space / P       pause
  + / −           game speed
