DAWN OF EMPIRE
An island at first light.

HOW TO PLAY
  Gatherers work on their own — berries, logs, and stone, whichever is scarce.
  Assign a job if you want someone locked to it. People are born in the huts
  when there is food and space. Elders pass only after a long life.
  Food is eaten every day. Trees grow back slowly. Yields vary.
  Farms need fertile ground, lumber camps a stand of pines, quarries a stone
  outcrop. Explore (X) to lift the fog; it returns if you leave.

TRIBES
  Redcliff and Ashfen start peaceful and will grow — or shrink — with their
  stores. Click a name, or T, to cycle who you trade with.
  Strike (or right-click their people) and every hunter marches together.

WEATHER
  Clear, mist, rain, storm, frost, golden light, flood, and drought.
  Floods raise the river. Droughts thin the yields. Storms pause building.

CAMERA
  WASD / arrows   pan
  Right-drag      rotate
  Wheel           zoom
  F               jump to your town hall
  1–8             choose a building
  T               cycle trade partner
  X               send people to explore
  I               cycle idle gatherers
  G               train a gatherer
  Space / P       pause
  + / −           game speed
