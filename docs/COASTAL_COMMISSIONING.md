# NPC coastal commissioning

The same slow 90-second coastal review can now fund one existing dock/hull chain for a society with an observed non-hostile foreign hall and dock. It retains eight healthy adult workers physically near home, 600 seconds of home meals, the working resource reserves, hull costs and a finite initial trade cargo. Unknown distant inventories never determine whether to build. Existing local dock placement checks knowledge, reachability, available materials and a valid shore; construction pays the ordinary 40-log cost and requires a real builder. A completed dock must have clear launch water connected to the observed foreign shore before paying for a hull.

Hull work calls `beginRiverboat` with a specific available adult, paying 40 logs and four stone for 150 seconds of bench labor. The existing paid project owns its costs/progress; the review resumes the same suspended project on an available replacement rather than repurchasing it. A real launched boat remains independent of its builder. The builder then physically walks back to the home cache under a protected loading assignment, where the next normal review can load existing cargo and paid voyage provisions. Direct interruption pauses this loading walk. Death never creates a launch, refund or delivery.

Optional saved `coastalCommissions` records are bounded to32, one per society, with a validated team/partner, actual dock/project/actor/boat IDs, phase, concrete waiting reason, and counts for reviews, blocked reviews, dock/hull payments, physical launches and cache arrivals. `Unit.coastalCommissionId` protects only the physical walk back to load; ordinary building and paid hull work keep their existing ownership contracts. Legacy absent fields preserve existing behavior.

Qualification: `scripts/coastal-trade.test.mjs` starts with eight original home adults, 220wood/60stone/500food, a fixed home hall and observed partner hall/dock, and no home dock or boat. The ordinary Game.step review places only a paid unfinished foundation, its builder completes the dock, paid hull crafting survives a mid-work save, and the same normal review eventually dispatches a physical trade voyage that returns once. Unrelated development reviews wait in this controlled fixture; no resources or buildings are granted after the start. Exact counters and movement are in `COASTAL_COMMISSION_SCENARIO_2026-10-31.json`. This qualifies the connected executor, not natural-map economic frequency.

Discovery dependency: the new controller never selects an unknown foreign hall. Existing `developRivalCoast` can fund a local dock without a known partner; `developRivalEquipment` can pay for one personal canoe; `tickScouts` allows canoe owners and `connectedForUnit` uses combined land/water navigation for them; moving scout bodies record firsthand cells. Therefore separate-island discovery is technically reachable through a paid canoe scout, rather than being strictly land-only. Its ordinary-campaign frequency, wide-ocean reliability and expedition provision model remain open qualification gates. No new free scout fleet or omniscient foreign target was added.
