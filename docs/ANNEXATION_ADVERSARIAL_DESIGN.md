# Independent annexation review

This is an adversarial test plan, not completed qualification. Runtime implementation belongs to the separate community-handover candidate. Source will be copied only after its owner declares a freeze.

The admission under test is public `settlePeace(game, team, 'annexation')`, with actual nearby witnessed military pressure and an existing known negotiation counterpart. Finite authored initial endowments and controlled topology are allowed for ownership isolation; they must be declared and must not be described as a native campaign proof. Production, hull and haulage inputs must be paid through their ordinary admission APIs. No completion, delivery or elapsed work is fabricated.

| Case | Initial physical state | Immediate assertion | Follow-through |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Paid food work | Actual paid cook and cereal batches, carrying/working/paused/returning phases | Same project/body/workplace IDs, paid inputs, progress and carried output; new legal owner; original maker origin | Strict save before any step; restore; ordinary completion and one delivery without another debit |
| Local stocks | Mixed food kinds, seeds, raw materials, items and earliest freshness | Exact aggregate quantity and kind, no conversion into provisions, no freshening, source stock drained only once | Repeated command rejected; decode round trip preserves totals |
| Craft culture | Distinct basket/pottery origins, active maker and stored output | Creator culture remains original, legal ownership changes through a dated record | Actual final delivery preserves origin and consumes no second input |
| Haulage/service | Paid paused sled/cart/hull-service work and actual input vehicle | Same paid progress and input vehicle, one owner and one actor claim | Resume ordinary work after restore; no duplicate vehicle/hull or replacement payment |
| Afloat community | Real paid hull, live captain and passengers, finite loaded goods and prior water distance | Hull and crew remain together at their actual coordinates; cargo/distance unchanged; no arrival/practice grant at command | Immediate strict save; ordinary sailing/unload counts only physical remaining travel/delivery |
| Husbandry | Real stored pen water and loaded shepherd water, live livestock | Liters unchanged; incoming/outgoing ownership accounts balance; no gathered-water receipt | Immediate strict save; ordinary consumption/delivery consumes the same liters once |
| Custody | Annexed-origin captive held by recipient; third-party captive held by annexed team; real escort IDs | Explicit release/adoption policy; origin history retained; guards and prisoner's finite provisions remain consistent | Immediate strict save and ordinary physical return/custody update |
| Historical work | Completed/lost jobs and diplomatic/knowledge history | Historical team/culture is not relabeled as a new accomplishment | Save remains valid; no extra practice or unseen-current knowledge |
| Rejected admission | Insufficient local pressure, unavailable counterpart, invalid draft | Entire nested live state unchanged except presentation banner | No resource debit, mutation, RNG draw, storage write or cache owner change |
| Forged provenance | Missing/wrong handover IDs, mismatched origins, wrong asset lists and broken chains | Strict decoder rejects each corrupted proof | Valid prior custody chain remains readable |
| Runtime reconstruction | Paid active claims plus selected stale entity refs and paths | Fresh committed state; current entity refs and coordinator claims reflect recipient ownership | Real command/step uses new objects, old owner holds no duplicated claim |

Evidence must separate the legal atomic ownership transition from subsequent physical work. Passing immediate decode is necessary but does not by itself prove eventual delivery, navigation or conservation. Any unsupported asset is a reported blocker rather than a reason to omit it silently. No acceptance percentage is claimed by this design.
