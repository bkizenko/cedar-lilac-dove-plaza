## Primary historical references and interpretation

- [American School of Classical Studies, Athenian Agora guide](https://www.ascsa.edu.gr/uploads/media/EnglishAMG-Sample.pdf) describes the Stoa of Attalos as colonnades backed by shop rooms. The original game market uses an open roofed arcade, three empty shop bays and fixed counters; no invented merchandise piles.
- [English Heritage, Jordan Hill Roman Temple](https://www.english-heritage.org.uk/visit/places/jordan-hill-roman-temple/history/) describes a central sanctuary/cella and surrounding columned portico. The game interpretation has an actual enclosed cella, doorway, freestanding portico columns and pitched tiled roof. It is an original readable civic kit, not a site reconstruction; the gable/proportions are art choices.
- [Acropolis Museum, gymnasium](https://www.theacropolismuseum.gr/en/node/1377) describes a central open courtyard, colonnades and educational activity. The learning-house interpretation has teaching wings, open courtyard, columns and empty permanent benches. No medieval university turret, fake scroll stock or abstract research machine is added. `university` remains the existing gameplay/save key; the display label is now Academy with the unchanged10-resident benefit.
- [English Heritage, Wroxeter research](https://www.english-heritage.org.uk/visit/places/wroxeter-roman-city/history/research/) records timber/clay construction in ordinary houses. [National Trust, Chedworth heritage record](https://heritagerecords.nationaltrust.org.uk/HBSMR/MonRecord.aspx?skin=printerfriendly&uid=MNA147601) documents courtyard/range architecture in an elite villa context. These inform future material/advanced-home choices; the current round hut is not relabeled a Roman villa.

## Actual grounding

The old full navigation-width/depth rectangular box is removed. Actual near-ground slices through model wall/column/leg geometry produce bearing traces; collinear triangle fragments are unioned once when shared kits are built. Only unsupported traces receive short masonry strips/pads sampled down into actual terrain. Flat ground emits zero supports. Courtyards and round-house centers stay open. Expanded warehouses use their actual repeated bay positions. Existing aligned gates/walls/bridges, planted fields and specialized terrain works retain their geometry/alignment. Keep geometry remains provisional and unchanged; its real bearing lines receive the same support treatment.

The rigid model base level uses actual bearing positions instead of unrelated navigation-rectangle corners. Footing cache still includes team, world, bank/height/sampler identity and actual terrain revision. Custom watchtower platforms retain their dedicated footprint/support logic. Real doorway metadata on hut/hall/temple can add bounded steps only where a grade difference requires them, wholly inside the unchanged collision rectangle. These are render supports, not a new walkable-interior/navigation system.

Initial capacity4096 grows only when required, capped131072 with an explicit overflow metric. Stable buildings neither resample terrain nor upload unchanged footing matrices; visibility/death/owner/site/world/height/cut changes invalidate correctly. No extra lights/shadows/textures are introduced. Current128² original albedo/roughness/bump maps are reused. The three new role meshes have market788,temple672,learning-house1008 triangles across the existing material families. Measured instance/geometry counts are not FPS claims.

Per-ID craftsmanship is deterministic and anchored below0.25m. The civic roof/post bases are not vertically separated by the old whole-model roof-height multiplier; subtle upper deviations supply variation instead. Roofs and support footprints remain inside existing society-transformed navigation rectangles.
