# Physically returned partner trade reports

A trader can learn a partner's offered goods and broad needs only while physically meeting at that partner's completed hall. The report contains five coarse need bands and the finite quote accepted or refused at that meeting; it never contains a remote treasury snapshot. Dispatch and cargo advice read only the originating society's own stores, observed halls and previously returned reports.

The same person carries the report home. A land trader publishes at the existing local cache arrival boundary; a coastal trader publishes after landing and reaching a real home supply point. Interrupted missions keep the report on the body. Death loses undelivered reports. Captivity withholds the report; release permits a later physical homecoming. Neither the captor nor the origin learns it remotely.

Tuning is explicit in `TRADE_INTEL_RULES`: 600 seconds of current advice, 1,800 seconds before expiry, at most four reports per carrier and 128 returned directed-pair reports. Arrival records a receipt date but never refreshes the original observation date. Late older reports cannot replace newer meetings. Unknown and stale markets remain exploratory, with no invented knowledge. Current reports discourage known unwanted cargo and favor observed needs. A bounded additional export target requires a reported demand plus a partner offer relieving a real local shortage.

Automatic traders now require an actual local import shortage and an actual export surplus protected by local working, advancement, unpaid construction and cold-fuel reserves. A society with all goods above reserve does not dispatch merely because a ratio looks attractive. Exchanges still use finite physically carried goods, local consent and the existing terms. Receiving anything is not evidence of economic profit: the separate natural-world qualification must measure useful shortage relief against operating provisions.

Metrics record meetings, refusals, returned reports, lost reports, expired arrivals and older ignored arrivals. Strict saves validate body ownership, dates, bounded quotes, unique directed reports and coarse bands, and reject injected exact stock fields.

Limits: this adds learned policy guidance, not guaranteed future terms, enforceable contracts, remote price broadcasts or a complete market model. Partner needs can change during travel. Existing player-directed exchanges remain under player control.
