# Witnessed local peace pressure

Candidate based on dee7005. This removes a causal information leak: the prior peacePressure counted all player and opposing adults globally. A remote newly spawned army could cause a society that had seen none of it to queue concessions or accept annexation.

Current coercion is measured around the opposing society’s own camp and finished town halls. Living free adults within55m provide local defense (condition-weighted1 for armed types/militia,0.25 for civilians; minimum denominator0.5). Only living armed player bodies within the community radius and within26m in three dimensions of an actual local opposing adult count as observed threats. People boarding, aboard or still disembarking a boat do not count as land forces until their actual boat attachment ends. Dead people, unreleased prisoners, children and ordinary unarmed workers do not create offensive pressure. The ratio and its components are a read-only measured report with named metre/condition parameters.

This models immediate physical leverage, not remembered army intelligence. Walking away ends that local leverage; already queued/delivered messages and historical reports are retained. Remote opposing reinforcements similarly do not count as local defense. The NPC only evaluates a new proposal when its normal peace-review timer permits; it does not rescan military pressure during the entire offer cooldown. Existing thresholds, actual provisioned diplomatic-message delivery, tribute carriers and ownership transfer executors remain unchanged.

Focused causal scenarios cover remote births/injuries/deaths with identical local reports and no concession, actual visible forces with strict saved queued messages, actual Game.step movement away while preserving the message history, unarmed bodies and high cliffs, a distant field encounter and remote opposing reinforcements, and a pure repeated query with unchanged full state and zero random draws. An additional actual boat boarding/landing Game.step scenario checks that physical attachment, rather than a fabricated crew flag or an instant troop spawn, gates pressure.

Existing peace economic fixtures now explicitly endow locally witnessed troops instead of using a remote army to force terms. The delivered-diplomacy case starts its finite military endowment near the actual opposing residents, then still requires the actual paid messenger to reach contact. Authored flat economic/control fixtures are not native campaign or paid army-recruitment evidence. The native browser fixture separately declares an initial settled enemy community, initial military group and revealed map for UI inspection only.

Limits: observation uses the game’s existing proximity convention, not new occlusion-aware line of sight. This does not add a naval blockade-pressure model, military intelligence report transport, all society-pair peace negotiation, a revised global-annexation asset-transfer contract or complete M2-07 information causality. Broader peaceful surrender/occupation/custody/empire mechanics retain their pending acceptance requirements. No additional whole acceptance gate is claimed. Final combined tests/build/browser evidence will be recorded in the integrated batch report.
