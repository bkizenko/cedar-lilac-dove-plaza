# V15 varied coastlines and region composition

New worlds use generation15. Older1–14 versions retain their existing generation path; loading a village preserves its terrain. New seeds vary layout family, substantial island count, positions, scale, orientation, inlet widths/depths and outline shape. Islands use ordinary, elongated, headland/bay, lobed and rugged profiles. The main island of rare mainland/satellite layouts remains allowed; mostly-land and divided continental maps still reach real edges/corners.

Within a map, silhouettes are measured using72 normalized radial samples. Pairwise RMS comparison includes every rotation and reflection, removes translation/scale, and rejects differences below0.045. Up to12 deterministic outline attempts avoid accepting copies. This is a polar silhouette measure, not exhaustive perceptual equivalence. Separate generated seeds are tested for distinct authoritative heightfield hashes; this does not claim mathematical uniqueness across all possible32-bit seeds. Reloading or deliberately repeating a seed must reproduce the same world.

One composed catchment supplies required mountains, hills, valleys, lake, rivers, canyon, cliff, terraces, plateau, pillars, dunes and waterfall. Other landmasses keep ordinary rolling ground and optional brooks, rather than repeating the full dramatic catalogue on every island. Eight substantial ecological regions, actual geometry coverage, usable camps, physical freshwater routes and above-average starting beauty remain checked. Coastline signatures and circularity are generation diagnostics; they add no per-frame render work.

V15 rejects unsuitable candidate terrain deterministically, with a maximum16 measured attempts. Requested seed, accepted terrain seed, attempt and rejection reasons are exposed in WorldData.generation. It does not flatten an unsafe start or lower geology thresholds to accept a seed. A duplicate start-route test formerly required straight lines despite connected dry paths; V15 now consistently uses the actual dry-cell route survey and retains the apron/depression/resident safety checks.
