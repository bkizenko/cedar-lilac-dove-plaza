# Paid rival tool upkeep

This parity extension awards no additional release gate. Player controls remain player-only. Shared repair admission accepts an optional fourth `team` argument, defaulting to zero; wrong-owner calls reject before payment. Non-player failures do not overwrite the player's banner.

Native strategy reviews tool upkeep before optional investment. Tunable `RIVAL_TOOL_UPKEEP_RULES` centralizes the 20-second review interval, 0.5-condition threshold, 360 seconds of meals and at least two other adult workers at home. One equipment worker is allowed at a time. New repairs preserve actual own material reserves, including unpaid plans and cold fuel. A completed actual farm is required. Builders, locked direct jobs, carried loads, rest, militia, protected missions and other committed actors are excluded. Existing paid repairs have precedence and reuse their saved materials/progress; they do not pay again or require a second capital reserve. Healthy tools are left alone. The shared executor still owns walking, physical benches, weather/night limits, labor and completion. Ordinary WorkBoard scheduling resumes when the actor finishes.

Cooldown state is bounded per-game/per-team runtime data, replaced when Game.state changes on reset/load. There is no new persisted schema or cross-frame stock cache. At most one eligible repair is admitted per review; there are no actor reservations for future repairs.
