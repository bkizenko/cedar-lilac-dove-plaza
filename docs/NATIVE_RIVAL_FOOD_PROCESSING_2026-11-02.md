## What is actually exercised

Three independent V14 pangaea / size1040 scenarios: seed345 society1, seed345 society2, and seed917 society1. The third seed was specified before its first run, rather than selected after searching for a successful camp. Terrain, rivers, lakes/waterbody arrays, features and native camp coordinates are generated by the ordinary Game.reset. Native trees, rocks, forage, fish and wildlife remain. The native five-worker opening positions are retained without relocation. The test does not flatten terrain, clear obstructions, override height/path queries, substitute camp coordinates, or repair topology after loading.

Before stepping, each tested society receives a declared finite starting inventory:35 prepared food,32 raw meat,32 logs,12 stone,100 drinking water, zero seeds and zero buildings. It retains five original generated adults. Player opening adults remain at their native positions with500 food and hold orders; other societies' bodies are removed as an explicitly controlled interaction setup. Tested workers begin holding with no locked jobs; ordinary NPC planning subsequently assigns their actual work. The opening is clear weather, quiet conflict and deferred external visitor/raid/birth events. This is native-geography/local-planner evidence, not an unmodified full campaign.

The automatic planner chooses and pays a reachable hearth foundation. The test saves only after actual construction is partially completed, then uses strict encode/decode with no world corrections. It next waits for a real fuel/raw-input-paid cooking batch to progress at the actual hearth, saves/loads again and requires exactly preserved progress, actor identity and stocks. Ordinary stepping then completes at least five cooked deliveries. All five original residents remain alive. Cold/rest/defense interruption is not qualified by these clear-weather daytime scenarios; the earlier focused fatigue/storm and ownership regressions remain separate evidence.

A SHA256 covers the actual height buffer, optional water-height buffer, water levels, water bodies, rivers, terrain features and native camps. It matches after each save/load and at the end. The external random stream is explicitly seeded and recorded (seed/draw count/end state) to reproduce ordinary planner decisions, resource/birth rolls and generated body attributes; no decision result is forced.

## Conservation and real competing activities

The test allows ordinary optional NPC development and native gathering. It therefore counts actual paid structures and physical timber/stone/food receipts instead of pretending that all starting capital remains reserved for cooking. The first hall's actual opening cost is queried before any construction; subsequent actual structures use their shared public cost function. The assertions cover initial logs + actual timber deliveries = paid buildings + cooking logs + actual warmth fuel + remaining logs, and the equivalent stone equation. Full food receipt/consumption/spoilage and drinking-water delivery/consumption/loss equations also close.

Native workers may harvest existing carcasses. A read-only per-step observer records real raw-carry parcels becoming delivered at the physical supply approach, including the case where an actual hall completion changes the camp cache during the same step. Recorded original/raw incoming parcels, recipe consumption, remaining raw and spoilage balance exactly. It does not command workers or add any stock. Production metrics are checked to belong solely to the tested society; other societies have no raw stocks. Cooked food is proven from completed paid projects and edible output metrics, separately from any native plant/animal food receipts.

An exploratory24-log opening succeeded in two cases but society2 spent competing capital on a real quarry and delivered four cooked batches before exhausting fuel. That was not a route defect, missing resource grant, or fifth completed batch. The final uniform32-log endowment is declared before all final scenarios start. A related balance observation remains: discretionary investment can consume the last fuel reserve. This artifact does not claim that five batches alone sustain the entire population indefinitely, nor that the native economy is difficult enough.
