# On-demand camera-frustum entity census

The Performance disclosure now has a **Count camera-view entities** button. It reports living player residents and present player structures, unique rendered body/model instances represented in the scene, and how many of their bounds intersect the current camera frustum. It records the village time and quality with the snapshot. It never reveals unknown rival population or structures.

The inspector reads actual instance matrices, the renderer's actual owner mapping, mesh counts/capacity, current camera transforms, ancestor/material visibility, camera layers and nonempty geometry. Dummy hidden bodies/roofs are excluded. Buildings with several parts or warehouse modules are deduplicated by owner ID. The artisan shader's small displacement is conservatively included in model bounds. The unit renderer retains its already-built grouping so a later state reorder cannot silently remap old matrices to new people. No extra per-frame census scan or sample timer is introduced.

This is a conservative body/model **bounds** test. It is not occlusion, pixels, shadows, accessories, or interface visibility: a counted model may be behind terrain, another model or the HUD. Terrain-deformed fields and excavations are explicitly excluded and their count is displayed. Foundations alone are not counted as a building model. Sheltered residents are excluded from body representation. The snapshot must be refreshed after camera or village changes.

The native V18 100/60 fixture was imported through the public UI in the production build at the isolated QA origin 8141. Close camera: 100/100 resident bodies and 37/60 structure models intersected. Ten ordinary outward wheel gestures: 100/100 and 60/60. Low quality preserved that result. Paused rendering was about 60 FPS. This validates the inspector's practical usefulness, not running performance acceptance.

A following running window began with that 100/60 view, but its final snapshot had 92/100 body bounds and 59/59 structure models. The actual displayed duration was 76 seconds, not the intended short sleep; tool latency extended it. Normal work continued throughout. It does **not** meet maintained 100/60 admission. The precise structure-change cause was not diagnosed. Running performance was 20.0 FPS, 84.3 ms frame p95, 13 rolling stalls over 100 ms, and 16.5 seconds of discarded catch-up. People/world CPU remained dominant. Four worker saves committed with zero synchronous fallback. No before/after speedup or acceptance credit is claimed.

Three focused geometry tests cover owner/capacity/deduplication, off-centre bounds intersection, camera/world transforms, hidden ancestors, layers, transparent/dummy/empty models, shelter admission and shader displacement. Typecheck and production build also pass. Exact UI numeric observations are preserved separately; private browser content is excluded. QA tab was paused and closed after capture.
