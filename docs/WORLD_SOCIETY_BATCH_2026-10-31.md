# V14 maps and independent societies

## Published scope

New worlds use generation 14. Saved worlds retain their recorded version and existing resources. Random generation weights are 55% multiple major islands, 20% edge-connected continent, 18% divided continents and 7% dominant mainland with satellite islands. These are weighted options, not a universal island-count rule. Continental land reaches actual edges/corners, with bounded decorative continuation. Surveyed usable land permits multiple separated towns on large landmasses.

The generator measures eight substantial connected biomes, relief, drainage, local water, dry resources, viable camps and above-average scenic starts. The test lab exposes the actual geographical features, connected biome areas and settlement surveys. Fresh camps have a known reachable freshwater bank inside normal initial exploration; automatic collection still walks and delivers finite water.

Seasonal wild plants now grow, ripen, deplete and decay by species. Visible produce follows the same stock consumed by ordinary residents, travellers and raiders. Existing marker-absent saves keep their previous forage. A full first-winter run exposed and corrected a raider harvest-accounting omission before publication.

Independent rival pairs can dispatch finite land raiding parties and paid peace messengers through actual movement/contact/return. Directed relations no longer require player involvement. This qualifies the controlled executors; natural-map frequency, full treaty economics and naval voyages remain separate work.
