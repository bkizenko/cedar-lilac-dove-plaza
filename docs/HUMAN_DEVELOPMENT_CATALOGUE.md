# Itemized human-development game catalogue

This is a design catalogue, not a claim that every historical development must occur in one linear sequence or that all these features already exist. It combines the user's requested survival/RTS/empire game with all accepted extensions. Current implementation status and acceptance are in `REQUEST_AUDIT.md`; the core overhaul scope is there. As explicitly requested on 2026-10-03, all 120 items, including formerly optional items, are committed overhaul scope. Inclusion does not mean implementation is complete. Features should create decisions and interact with existing labor/material/knowledge systems; omit decorative inventory clutter. Preserve ongoing saved settlements through compatible additions.

3. Uneven finite mineral deposits and persistent ore grade.
4. Local shortages that encourage exploration, exchange or migration.
5. Forest recruitment, succession, clearing and regrowth.
6. Grassland grazing, woodland wild food and upland extraction strategies.
7. Rivers, lakes, wetlands, fishing grounds and navigable channels.
8. Flood, drought, wildfire and storm risks with warning and recovery.
9. Carrying capacity and depleted hunting/gathering grounds.
10. **Accepted extension:** longer climate shifts and settlement adaptation.

## Execution grouping

The survival, physical economy, believable information and rival behavior foundations come first. Extend the current architecture and keep existing worlds intact. Complete the committed items/acceptance from `REQUEST_AUDIT.md` in ordered phases; this catalogue is the requested broader list for deciding future scope. Do not attempt to implement all optional candidates at once, nor count a menu label as a completed system.

For each selected item record: decision it creates, material/labor/information inputs, observable output, interactions, save schema, AI use, player controls and acceptance test. The same mechanic should often serve several items: cargo supports gathering/trade/ruins/raids; local stores support preservation/hauling/war; practical knowledge supports tools/ages/culture; settlement identity supports migration/administration/secession.

### Historical interpretation boundaries

Farm work has seasonal peaks: sowing/ploughing, tending and harvest, plus storage, repairs and livestock care. Fields need visible scheduled work, not endless daily tilling. Soil potential is distinct from depletion/fallow recovery.

Vitruvius inspires site suitability, drainage, water access, prevailing winds and street orientation. Represent measurable exposure, smoke, water contamination and comfort; ancient explanations about unhealthy air are not modern medical facts.

Irrigation and concentrated fertile corridors create coordination costs, surplus and strategic bottlenecks. Rugged terrain increases travel costs and can encourage local autonomy or bargaining. Institutions also depend on history, technology and incentives: never hardcode river societies as despotic or mountain societies as democratic.

References: [Vitruvius Book I](https://lexundria.com/vitr/1/gw), [medieval calendar agriculture, Metropolitan Museum](https://blog.metmuseum.org/artofillumination/2010/03/10/calendar-pages/), [Mesopotamian water-management research](https://wires.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/wat2.1230).


### Additional continuation requests

- Larger mountain chains, broad hills, basins/valleys, lakes and multi-direction rivers. Sheltered valleys can support fertile land and reduced wind exposure; do not universally make valleys warm (night-time inversions can occur). Distinguish soil, drainage and flood risk.
- Varied river widths and navigable reaches; boats should carry more cargo and travel faster than overland transport. Foot trade has meaningful travel cost and scarcity-priced benefits. River navigation/boats and this travel rebalance remain outstanding.
- Foreign explorers and actual visiting traders. Visiting traders now carry owned cargo and negotiate at the player's village; foreign explorers and independent knowledge maps remain outstanding.
- Village rise, survival, collapse, refugees and refounding remain a required major phase, not implemented by terrain changes or visitor missions. The present fixed major factions must later support multiple persistent settlements with stable identities, ownership and save migration.
- Never announce undiscovered settlement counts. Larger territory halos and colorful fading edges; village priorities should reset current work assignments, not only future jobs. Villager cycling belongs in the selection panel.
- Gradual hunger weakness, death after roughly a month without food, recovery and personal food needs. Current season calendar uses a compressed 1800-second year; day/night time and annual day labels still use a different scale and require deliberate harmonization rather than silently changing all saved timers.
