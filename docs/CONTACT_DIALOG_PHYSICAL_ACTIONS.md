# Compact meetings and physical contact actions

The initial meeting offers Talk and Trade. Conversation topics show the person's recorded response inside the meeting; a person carrying goods offers a follow-up to discuss their bundle. Other choices contains coercion, dismissal and withdrawal without a default wall of disabled requirements. Choosing an unavailable action returns its current reason without inventing force, supplies or consent.

When an existing free adult is between 3 and 12 metres away, Walk closer to exchange issues an ordinary saved move order to the meeting. The simulation resumes behind the open meeting. Neither bundle changes owners during travel. The player confirms a separately revalidated exchange after arrival; then both actual carriers must bring their goods home. Changing targets, duties or cargo can invalidate an offer. Paid work, custody, boat and protected journey owners are not commandeered for this approach.

Trade shows current carrying capacity from the real actor's equipment, existing relationship trust, and visible nearby hostile and armed people. It does not disclose foreign inventories or hidden troops. Current pack/sled/canoe capacity comes from the existing haulage model; this batch neither creates nor equips transport.

A visitor within 35 metres of an actual village supply drop may be approached by existing free armed adults within 150 metres instead of requiring them already inside the former 50-metre contact ring. Routes remain physically checked. A demand dispatches the nearest available empty-handed guards, up to four, based on visible defenders and the existing two-to-one surrender rule. The first guard waits for dispatched companions to arrive before making the demand. Resistance still causes actual hostility/combat; no click guarantees surrender, creates equipment or remotely deposits goods. These are adjustable `CONTACT_RULES` values.

## Verification

Twenty-three distinct focused checks passed across `contact-actions.test.mjs` and `encounters.test.mjs`. They include physical capture/combat/refusal, finite bundle exchange/delivery, save/reload, protected children and commitments, bounded personal reports, and compact rendered controls. New cases prove actual carrier approach before confirmation, an existing guard's 94-metre response, a two-guard physical rendezvous, and a waiting visitor's return of the same live body and finite cargo to its actual home. Controlled terrain fixtures isolate these mechanics; they are not natural-campaign acceptance evidence. Desktop visual review remains the integrator's gate.

## Remaining scope

Ordinary visitors already retain their body/cargo through their physical return; no visitor deletion was added. Automatic risk-based trade escort planning, loading a different offer from stores, and choosing/equipping a pack or sled from this meeting are not implemented here. Existing escort and haulage systems remain separate.

Sea-raider persistence is an unresolved independent issue: `seaRaidAI` currently zeroes carried loot and sets the actor's health to zero on reaching its retreat landing. Replacing that with a real retained boat/home journey requires changes outside this contact batch. This dialog does not claim to solve sea-raider persistence or a complete maritime trading campaign.
