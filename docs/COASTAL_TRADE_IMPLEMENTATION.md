# Physical NPC coastal trade

This batch adds a bounded ordinary review for existing owned NPC riverboats. It selects a free adult physically at the home supply cache, retains four other adults, observes a foreign hall and dock, verifies connected water and dry landing routes, and loads finite existing cargo and paid food/water. Foreign stock is consulted only at the hall after actual arrival. Existing local barter pricing/reserves execute the exchange; the same person boards the same moored vessel and carries the result back to the home cache before stock is credited.

The boat remains the existing paid five-seat riverboat: 40 logs, 4 stone and 150 seconds of bench work. The voyage controller does not create free vessels, move a person ashore, grant exchange rewards, consume distant home meals, or convert ownership. The bounded commissioning controller can now pay existing dock construction and hull crafting executors for an observed trading opportunity. Captains remain protected from ordinary work, recruitment and scouting. A direct order pauses the saved voyage with its cargo and remaining provisions; resumption does not buy a replacement pack. A lost person cannot grant a return reward; a surviving stranded person retains its pack. Capture transfers only the custody pack's available physical capacity; remaining abandoned provisions remain recorded on the lost voyage. No naval combat is claimed.

The saved optional `coastalVoyages` records own actor/boat IDs, observed endpoints, phase/resume phase, remaining provisions, finite goods and measured movement/boarding/landing/consumption/delivery counters. `coastalTradeReviewAt` bounds planner frequency to 90 seconds after the initial 120 seconds. `Unit.coastalVoyageId` protects the actor and excludes its carried provisions from duplicate home food/water consumption. Legacy absent fields preserve ordinary behavior. The strict save validator checks references, single actor/boat ownership and required metrics.

`dispatchCoastalTrade`, `resumeCoastalTrade`, `tickCoastalTrade`, `coastalTradeAI`, `reviewCoastalTrade` and `validCoastalTradeState` are exported by `src/game/coastalTrade.ts`. Game.step calls the real controller and executor. Boat commands have optional explicit owner-team/internal-mission arguments; their player defaults are unchanged.

Focused qualification: `scripts/coastal-trade.test.mjs` constructs a declared disconnected-shore fixture with fixed halls/docks, original adults, finite starting inventories and firsthand observed partner knowledge. It builds the boat through paid bench work using Game.step, lets the ordinary voyage review dispatch, saves a paused captain at sea, rejects a corrupt actor reference, resumes the same voyage and checks exact seller/home goods conservation and no repeated credit. `docs/COASTAL_TRADE_SCENARIO_2026-10-31.json` records its measured outcome. All six legacy riverboat tests also run.

Known-opportunity commissioning/dock acquisition is covered by the separate commissioning scenario. Open gates: natural-map commissioning and voyage frequency, unknown-shore discovery reliability and long-route survival; player merchant contracts aboard multi-seat boats; passenger/escort fleets; naval combat and interception. The controlled crossing qualifies the connected executor, not those broader systems or campaign balance.
