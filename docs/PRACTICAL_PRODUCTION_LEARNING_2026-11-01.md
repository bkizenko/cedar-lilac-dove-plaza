# Practical production and informal teaching

This is bounded hands-on learning for the existing23 exact processing recipes, not a completed technology tree or institutional education system. People retain mixed work roles.

Actual staffed progress records its contributors. Only delivery of useful output records a successful demonstration; discarded firing shards do not. Pickup of somebody else’s dried goods does not make the carrier its maker. Existing saved stocks supply observations without inventing prior work history. Personal exact-method practice adds2.5% per own successful batch or1.25% per taught batch, capped at20%; ingredients, travel, fuel and physical slots remain necessary.

After3 actual successful batches of the same exact method, a maker can teach one other available adult at their working batch. The learner physically walks to the workplace and observes the original maker’s staffed work for half the base work or30seconds, whichever is lower. Waiting for finished delivery releases the learner for other tasks. Credit requires actual useful output delivered, sufficient observed work and a qualifying teacher contribution. Pausing, failed/lost batches, unattended curing and merely reading the panel grant no taught success. Moving pauses observation and releases its actor reservation; resuming requires the original paid batch.

The Knowledge panel reports cooking, milling, baking, preservation, hides, garments, pottery and apprenticeship from actual materials, paid projects, demonstrated deliveries and adoption. Forming greenware alone does not prove finished pottery; wearing real clothing or delivering with a real jar supplies relevant adoption evidence.

Strict optional save fields retain exact methods, contributor work, teacher/learner references and bounded observed times. Legacy saves remain valid with unknown history. Workload includes active observation/travel, excludes paused lessons and frees adults waiting for delivery. Stored paid batches remain visible as paused future work, with no fictitious active maker or reserved workstation.

Executed evidence:4 natural-terrain component scenarios in scripts/production-practice.test.mjs pass: physical repeated production and teaching/save/load/interruption, side-effect-free knowledge/workload, strict corrupt-evidence rejection, and distinct hands-on maker versus pickup carrier. Inputs used for these component scenarios are explicitly endowed; no campaign or full institutional-education gate credit is implied.
