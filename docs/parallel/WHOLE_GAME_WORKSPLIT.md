## Model research informing the split (November11)

Official OpenAI guidance places GPT6Astra on ambiguous/deep work, GPT6.1Sol on complex time/cost-conscious work, and cheaper models on scoped edits: https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/model-selection . Anthropic describes Opus5.5 as stronger for complex open-ended judgment and Sonnet5.5 as a faster scoped-work complement: https://www.anthropic.com/claude-sonnet-5-5 and https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/sonnet-5-5/overview . xAI describes Grok4.7 as its coding/agentic flagship and publishes vendor benchmarks: https://x.ai/news/grok-4-7 . These are vendor claims and differing harnesses, not proof of best Hearthwild performance or3x end-to-end throughput. Account access to specific models is unverified.

Recommendation: use an available strong Claude model for the international systems track, scoped lower-cost execution where appropriate, and keep Grok on easy reversible text/catalogue work according to the user's preference. Compare delivered passing changes per time/usage on this repo before widening ownership. Do not automatically change the selected model or buy API credits.
