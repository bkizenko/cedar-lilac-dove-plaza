## What the user actually wants

A beautiful, detailed, original historical settlement/strategy world in which people, buildings and landscapes explain how life and work happen. Dawn of Man informs subsistence and believable work; Civilization informs dense cities, material culture and broader scope; Age of Empires informs legible functional buildings and responsive commands. The world must remain enjoyable and playable on the user's M1 MacBook Air with 8 GB shared memory.

The chosen H direction is a full world/art interpretation: real geometry, surfaces, lighting, varied buildings, people and visible actions. It is not satisfied by recoloring the current primitive models. Keep E available as the exact comparison direction. The user wants implemented 3D samples and progress visible in the running game, with source candidates, generated illustrations, native acceptance and published content distinguished explicitly.

## Feedback that must not be lost

- Reject glossy, polished mobile/microtransaction presentation: shiny ornament, commerce-like badges, decorative currency counters and blurred backdrops. Desktop strategy clarity and tactile materials are the reference.
- Reject a palette-only repaint presented as a complete H transformation. Add convincing form, material, scale, construction joints, work and atmospheric depth.
- Reject universal raised rectangular building platforms. Ground walls, floors and entrances in actual terrain. A hearth is stones/fire; a field is planted terrain, not a shed on a plinth. Genuine retaining walls, stilts, terraces or structural foundations are allowed where the site and building need them. This does not mean flattening H's carved terrain contours.
- Reject identical huts with small color/height changes presented as structural variety. Homes within one culture must have meaningful coherent differences.
- Reject generic house bodies for every workshop, market, temple and university. Purpose must read from structure and circulation at normal play zoom.
- Reject flat, monotonous terrain and smooth tall mounds as the full landscape solution. Mountains, nested relief, rock strata, usable rolling foreground, rivers/lakes, coherent regional transitions and rare exceptional vistas matter.
- Reject fake visual completion: no full-looking storage when empty, imaginary logs/meat/animals, free perpetual fires, clean new buildings labeled ruins, unstaffed decorative work, or portable canoes worn on heads as ordinary sailing.
- Reject claims that a concept image, source mesh, component test or single model preview proves integrated gameplay quality or smooth crowds.

## Architecture: function, period, culture and individual variation

Use **function × material × learned practice × era × prosperity × stable building variation**. First-release families include earthen courtyards, timber halls/work yards, stone terraces, and reed/coastal structures. Each needs recognizable homes, stores, production sites, civic buildings and defenses. Trade, migration and apprenticeships can introduce techniques gradually. Climate and available material constrain form, but culture is learned history rather than a fixed biome/ethnicity label.

Research every production building family using at least one identified real structure, photo or archaeological reconstruction. Record source, date/region, construction/material logic, functional spaces and uncertainty. These sources guide original forms; a reference photo is not automatically licensed as a game texture. Current documentation does not establish complete catalogue provenance.

Within one family vary footprint/dimensions, roof axis/pitch/geometry, bay count, door/window placement, open frontage, courts, annexes, repairs, wear, material batches and makers' details. Keep cultural coherence, straight usable entrances, real standing/work spaces, terrain contact and truthful collision. Generate deterministically from saved seed/site/materials/household history; save identity and generator version rather than reroll on load. Stone is not universally a late-era unlock, and multi-storey limits depend on construction.

Use the player's preferred sandstone/plaster/courtyard/waterworks language, informed by Nabataean Petra, for an appropriate evolving city. Prosperity first improves upkeep, structure and services; galleries, carving and exceptional ornament follow selectively. Do not sprinkle arbitrary pagoda roofs onto unrelated ordinary buildings. Original combinations can draw from Nile/desert-canyon, European, Chinese, Pacific and Indigenous practices without assigning every invented culture to one modern nationality.

Existing research leads, already recorded locally: Skara Brae (Historic Environment Scotland), Mesa Verde (NPS), vernacular changes (Historic England), Petra, Karnak, Provins, Dujiangyan, Rapa Nui and Cahokia. These are leads from prior project research, not newly verified research in this handoff. Follow the actual linked records: [architecture evidence and variation](../INCENTIVE_FIRST_MASTER_PLAN.md#L494); [construction families and Petra](../INCENTIVE_FIRST_MASTER_PLAN.md#L176); [civic structural reference table](../SETTLEMENT_ECONOMY_AND_ART_PLAN.md#L50).

Before replacing a registered role, inspect [37-role visual inventory](../BUILDING_VISUAL_AUDIT_2026-11-02.md). It distinguishes existing useful models from weak silhouettes; source presence is not visual acceptance. Preserve functional hearth/butcher/smokehouse/barracks/fibre geometry unless a reviewed change improves it. The recorded six-model material candidate is a bounded historical batch, not evidence that all 37 buildings or all culture families are complete.

## Materials, terrain and visible life

Use construction logic and actual state to drive detail: timber grain and bracing, layered thatch/reed, wattle/earth, irregular masonry, plaster, pottery, exposed rock/soil strata, damp edges, worn paths, real crop stages and harvest stubble. Regional macro-form, middle-scale cliffs/banks/vegetation and restrained fine material variation work together. Texture alone cannot repair bad proportions or terrain. Shared roughness/normal/albedo treatment should support H's tactile warmth rather than glossy plastic.

Terrain composition draws from Bierstadt/Cole: framed foreground, illuminated valleys, winding water, layered ridges, atmospheric distance, occasional waterfalls/gorges. Ordinary places remain common, exceptional scenery is rare, and beauty is independent of fertility/buildability. Keep actual wet/dry, water level, navigation and rendered surface consistent; Grok supplies those authoritative constraints. Dawn of Man/Civ references support readable work yards, textured ground/rock, blue-depth water, clear banks and integrated city density. No HOI4 art influence is recorded.

Animate the real task: idle, walk/carry, rowing, combat, threshing strike, winnow-basket tilt, quern sweep, bread peel, animal tending and construction stages. Props attach to actual equipment, owned cargo, paid inputs and progress. Visible logs, baskets, ores, tools, jars, garments, armor and hulls must match simulation state and disappear/change correctly at distance. Construction should reveal actual structure/scaffolds/material stages. Ruins need broken/aged remains and finite discoverable artifacts/history.

Names, symbols, flags and palette persist on relevant diplomacy surfaces, territorial flags, ships, armor and civic buildings. Clothing varies appropriately with season, local material and era. Armor should read by equipment/protection type before faction dye, then vary weave, bindings, plates, helmets, shield motifs, makers, quality and wear. Ornament must not imply protection that mechanics do not provide. Source: [materials, animation and hardware plan](../SETTLEMENT_ECONOMY_AND_ART_PLAN.md#L77); [identity/armor requirements](../INCENTIVE_FIRST_MASTER_PLAN.md#L494).

## Minimal desktop UI and audio

Desktop only; no mobile layout/touch acceptance work. Keep the map central and commands stable. Slim resource/ready-food quantities, compact icons with purpose/cost tooltips, short paid queues/progress, one precise blocker with remedy, and contextual actions replace walls of text. Seeds remain measurable in detail rather than masquerading as edible food in the compact bar. Inventory/capacity must be simple and truthful. Knowledge uses short methods/evidence; work panels show phase, worker and actual next action. Ledger tabs hold live history/production and seasonal comparisons; routine reports arrive at season end. Trade/contact uses actual leader, known stakes and real goods, not hidden omniscient stocks.

Preserve keyboard controls, pause, camera movement while nonmodal panels are open, hotkeys and map clickability. The existing study proposes readable text, contrast, few interactions and an unobstructed idle map; it does not authorize reducing functionality to decorative icons. UI layout/art direction is Claude's; queue/inventory/accessibility implementation is Codex's; map/placement controls are Grok's.

Audio should support calm ordinary activity and real attack/defense states, with skip music and spatial/rate-limited work/combat sounds. Do not trigger battle music for nonexistent raids. Historical calmer/acoustic preferences supersede older blanket dramatic-conquest requests; adaptive state music remains part of the current full handoff. Do not create a unique high-resolution material/mesh/rig for every resident or building. Quality changes preserve identity. Keep approved concepts outside the game bundle. No full engine migration during reliability work; an isolated measured future benchmark is distinct from a migration decision.

A GLB preview or software-rendered browser cannot prove hardware performance. The frozen full-handoff gate proposes median <=33ms and p95 <=50ms at 100 visible residents/60 buildings; the art plan also records more ambitious 60Hz/300-person aspirations. These are different proposed tests, not interchangeable achieved claims. Codex must resolve any budget conflict explicitly rather than silently changing the frozen release gate.
