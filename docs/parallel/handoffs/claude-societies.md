## Before → after
Before: rivals funded a hull only after observing a foreign hall (coastalCommission), so a society with an unknown coast never went to sea; the old personal-canoe equipment path could be blocked by one injured canoe owner.
After: every 120 s each living rival reviews its OWN knowledge grid (`rivalKnowledge` cells + live own-unit/building cells). If unknown water adjoins known water on the same connected water body and no partner hall is yet known, it:
1. reuses an idle owned hull moored at a finished own dock, or pays dock (via `developRivalCoast`) and hull (via `beginRiverboat` → haulage bench) from actual stores; builder labor is real; no free hull, no carried boats;
2. musters 2–3 healthy free adults (≥60% hp, not thirsty, not injured/prisoner/dependent/militia/committed/carrying), keeping 3 adults home; the injured canoe owner is simply ineligible and blocks nothing;
3. each crew member walks to an actual cache and loads a finite `scoutProvisions` pack (420 s budget, keeps 450 s home meals/180 s water), walks to the hull and boards;
4. sails legs toward the nearest unknown frontier cell (`commandRiverboat`; sailing/rowing physics untouched), recording only cells within 40 m sight of the hull, coast cells, plausible landings and halls within the settlement witness radius;
5. turns home on: no frontier (`surveyed`), 6 legs, 420 s, provisions below return-time+60 s, foe within 24 m, blocked route, or owner recall;
6. lands at the recorded dry home landing, walks to the cache, refunds leftover pack food/water through `returnScoutPackAtCache`, and only then commits the report cells into that society's `rivalKnowledge`.
Rejection reasons are counted per society in `state.seaExploration.teams[].reasons` (partner-known, water-preparing, no-frontier, no-reachable-water, no-hull-materials, no-builder, hull-pending, no-crew, no-provisions, home-meals, hull-lost, crew-lost, route-blocked, hull-reassigned, cancelled) and mirrored to `recordCoastalReview(...,'sea-explore',...)`. Retry after 600 s on finish.

## Shared contracts
- IDs: voyage id from `g.id()`; `Unit.seaExplorationId` ↔ active voyage `crew` (minus `home`/`left`). One open voyage per team. Boat ownership never changes; `seaExplorationOwnsBoat` makes coastal trade refuse that hull; coastal commissioning waits while a sea hull is being paid.
- Crew death: dead member's pack moves to an adjacent living mate within pack limits; remainder booked `foodLost/waterLost`. Capture: pack handed to the prisoner via existing scoutProvisions captivity rule. All crew lost → `lost`, hull retained, report not committed.
- Hull loss: survivors try to wade to a connected dry shore; after 120 s without one they are released (recalled, pack kept) and the voyage is `lost` with failure `hull-lost`, report unconfirmed.
- Cancel: `cancelSeaExploration(g,id)` — commission/ready: paid dock/hull stay owned; mustering: land and unload packs; outbound: sail home (`recall`). Direct interrupt of an ashore member releases only that member (`pauseSeaExploration`).
- Conservation ledger per voyage: `foodLoaded = foodReturned + foodUsed + foodLost + foodWithPeople` (same for water), asserted in tests.

Behavior:
- Parcels `{id,kind,amount,food?,x,z,originalOwnerTeam,fromTeam,droppedAt,claimedBy?,lastReason?}` are created only by moving a dead carrier's exact `carry` (any team). Same-spot, same-kind, same-owner unclaimed parcels merge (so a parcel can exceed one carry). Nothing is created or destroyed; `ledger{dropped,pickedUp,deposited,...}` records flows.
- Original owner: `CargoMark{unitId,owner}` written by `markStolenLoad` at the two raider theft sites and on pickup from a parcel; unmarked goods belong to the carrier's own team.
- Recovery: every 3 s the nearest free healthy (>=60% hp) adult of the owner within 70 m of the parcel (parcel within 160 m of a cache) is dispatched; it walks (`steer`), picks up min(parcel, carry capacity), walks to the nearest real cache (`supplyDrops`) and deposits through the same stock path as ordinary hauling; large parcels take several trips. Eligibility excludes prisoners, militia, dependents, jobLock, emergency, missions, expeditions, trade, boats, task commitments. If a higher-priority duty takes the unit the trip is dropped with reason `busy` and the claim released; carried goods stay in hands and the normal return-and-deposit AI finishes the job.
- Bounded pursuit: owner adults within 30 m of a living thief holding their goods (max 3 per thief) chase for 40 s / 60 m from where they started / until below 45% hp, attack when in reach, then stand down (rest 120 s) back to prior idle/hold/gather work. Reasons recorded: pursuit-time, pursuit-distance, pursuit-hurt, pursuit-ended. Note the existing defense system also reacts once a chaser strikes (raiseAttackAlarm → emergency); the trip then ends `busy` and defense owns the fight, which is intended.
- Finders keepers: another team's free adult within 18 m takes an unclaimed parcel after 240 s of owner grace and deposits it in its OWN cache (`depositedByFinder`). Raider team 3 does not pick up parcels.
- Rejection/abandon reasons (global counters in `droppedCargo.reasons`, last reason per parcel): no-owner-adult, too-far, unreachable, no-cache, claimed, not-eligible, busy, walk-timeout, return-timeout, parcel-gone, carry-full, pursuit-*, claimant-lost. Parcels never expire or vanish.
- Save: `state.droppedCargo` optional (absent = none; old saves load unchanged); `validDroppedCargoState` runs inside the existing `validCoastalTradeState` path (versions, kinds, amounts > 0, owners/units exist, claim <-> trip back-references, bounds).

## TRADE-NEEDS / G15 (status: source pushed, not merged)
Files: new `src/game/tradeNeeds.ts`, `scripts/trade-needs.test.mjs` (12 tests). Wiring (same seam as sea exploration / stolen cargo): `coastalTrade.ts` (review, tick, per-unit AI, validator — 4 one-line hooks), `workCommitments.ts` (`tradeNeedsId` registered as a committed task).

Behavior:
- Goods (only what a society actually holds): resources food/wood/stone/copper/iron, weapons spears/bows/blades, armor paddedArmor, tools cordage/basket, artifacts pottery/cloth, animals goat (real `Livestock`). Need = reserve − stock (existing `rivalTradeReserve` for resources; adult/pop-derived reserves for the rest); surplus = stock − reserve. Proposals pair a real need with a real surplus; a kind-mix penalty rotates offers; per-society `mix` and `categories` counters are the measurement (`tradeNeedsDiagnostics`).
- Proposal moves nothing. The partner answers after a distance-based delay (NPC) or via `answerTradeOffer` (player; unanswered offers expire after 600 s and count as a refusal). NPC partner accepts only if it needs the offered good and has surplus of the requested one. On acceptance both sides' goods are reserved into escrow atomically (proposer re-checked: surplus, carriers, home adults, pen room for animals); failure rolls back with nothing created or destroyed.
- Carriers: living free adults (pack-animal handlers first, capacity per kind from `carryingCapacity` for resources, per-carrier lots otherwise), up to 3, must walk to a real cache to load, walk to the partner hall seen on a real visit, are paid from the partner's escrow at the hall, walk home and deposit. Receipts: loaded / paid / delivered / refunded / lost. Resource cargo is mirrored on `unit.carry` so a dead carrier drops an existing stolen-cargo parcel; any carrier loss before payment aborts the offer (survivors return what they hold, the partner's escrow is refunded). Animals are led as escrowed `Livestock` and enter a pen within 40 m of the receiving hall.
- Escorts: count (0-3) from observed risk (trust from this pair's completed/lost history, raiders within 60 m of the party, distance, cargo value, foe status). Escorts are existing free militia, otherwise workers armed from real stock via `equipCaravan` at the cache (unarmable escorts drop out; `escortsArmed` recorded).
- Declines: cooldown per (from, partner, give, get) doubling to 1 h; amount shrinks x0.6 per decline; refusals teach the proposer ("did not need X", "could not spare Y") so later reviews pick different goods. End-to-end test shows two complementary societies converging on a completed trade on their own review cycle.
- Markets (read from finished `market` buildings only): review interval / (1 + 0.6 x markets up to 3); spread 0.30 − 0.07 x (own + partner markets), floor 0.04. Tested: more proposals per hour and narrower realized spread with markets.
- Save: `state.tradeNeeds` optional (absent = none; old saves load); `validTradeNeedsState` inside `validCoastalTradeState` (versions, kinds, phases, bounded arrays, unique ids, carrier/escort back-references both ways, escrow only on reserved active offers).
Not done / limits: player as proposer; no messenger unit carries the proposal (delay only); NPC caravans do not yet use boats (coastalTrade handles that separately); armor/weapons are traded as stock counts (not individual items with wear/quality); loaned escort weapons leave stock while on the road (conservation tests count tradeable goods, not loaned arms); the goat must have a pen near the receiving hall.
Interface requests: **Claude Experience** — caravan visuals: read `state.tradeNeeds.offers[].phase/lines/carriers/escorts` (party strength = escorts.length, goods = lines), pack goats via `unit.haulage==='pack'`; trade dialog for the player: list `offers` with `to===0 && phase==='proposed'` and call `answerTradeOffer(g,id,accept)`. **Codex** — sign-off on the shared fields above; if a canonical market/price hook is wanted, replace `marketCount`/`tradeSpread` (two exported functions in tradeNeeds.ts) rather than editing pricing here.

## G16 sea storms (status: source pushed, not merged)

- Save: optional `state.seaStorms`, validated in the coastal-trade validator.
Interface requests: **Grok** — canonical deep-water extents (this module uses `waterAt - height >= 2.5 m`); **Codex** — weather owner may replace the `weather==='storm'` gate with a dedicated sea-state event; hull-physics owner may want storm drag instead of damage-driven slowdown.
