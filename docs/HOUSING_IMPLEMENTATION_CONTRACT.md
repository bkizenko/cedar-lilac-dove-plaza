# Paid timber homes: implementation and bounded evidence

This isolated change is based on the parent-provided `62a122e + V19 C2 + grounded civic B` snapshot. B/C2 terrain and art exports remain unchanged. Parent integration, production build and actual desktop review are required before publication; this document grants no release gate credit.

## Actual behavior

Both flows use the same saved quote: **24 logs, 8 stone, 6 `Tribe.items.plantFibre`, 48 person-work-seconds**, plus actual hauling in 8-unit packets. Values are abstract inventory units, not kilograms. Named controls are in `housingRules.ts`; paid projects retain their original quote and duration.

An existing completed hut remains a five-place usable shelter throughout its upgrade. Only completion converts that same building ID to `house`: unchanged 5.4×5.2 collision footprint, seven places, 320 maximum HP. Absolute existing HP and maintenance condition are preserved: upgrading a damaged 150-HP hut yields a 150-HP home, not a repair. A completed home adds 1.5°C only when the existing physical shelter test actually selects that home; distant residents and unfinished upgrades receive no extra protection.

The builder reaches a real communal cache before an atomic resource/item payment. Paid inputs remain in three authoritative buckets: uncollected at the original source, delivered at the site, and one finite packet on its actual carrier or at its actual drop coordinates. `Unit.housingCargo` is a validated serialized view of that one packet, not another inventory. Per-material quote = uncollected + delivered + packet. Actual carrying load affects existing walking speed. One coordinator actor claim and one site workspot prevent simultaneous builders from spending/work-crediting the same project.

Pause, direct-order interruption and actor death retain/drop the packet in place. A replacement must walk to collect it. Strict carrying/working saves reconstruct claims without charging again. Immediate Pause after actual coordinator reconstruction explicitly releases that exact saved housing claim, even before the construction adapter audit. A failing-before/passing-after regression preserves this case. Target removal preserves the paid buckets and progress as an inactive retained project, with no remote refund. An unfinished paid new home cannot also generate a normal salvage refund from those retained materials. Recovering an entirely destroyed project's retained inputs is not a new playable recovery command in this batch.

Ownership is recorded independently for site, source escrow and current packet. Partial capture can leave them with different owners, blocking use of foreign materials without deleting them. Whole-community handover moves each actual owned bucket and its people/sites; original payer and personal method provenance remain historical. High/Normal/Low construction priorities, pending builder queues and workload forecasts include renovations and their remaining hauling. Paused work is excluded from active allocation.

## Learning and controls

The selected hut offers **Improve home**, its actual quote, progress and pause/resume. A completed paid home offers **Try home**: an available adult must walk to its actual approach and spend 30 seconds using/observing it, with normal needs active. This records a dated personal method; there are no research points. Other residents learn by performing their own actual use, not a proximity-based instant teaching credit.

The build menu shows the locked timber-home option with its explicit requirements. After a living resident demonstrates the prototype, placement creates a real unpaid `house` plan. Only a builder who personally knows the method can execute it; materials, physical transport and work are still required. The Knowledge panel reports need → actual paid prototype → actual use → personal adoption, and provides the same real commands.

Native NPC review is bounded to once per 15 simulated seconds. It requires genuine crowding/cold need, food and working-material reserves, existing finite fibre, a real hut/cache and available builders. The ordinary shelter planner prefers the learned class only when those actual inputs and a living adopter exist. No NPC receives fibre or knowledge. The pre-existing bast-harvest entrypoint remains player-directed; this batch does not claim natural autonomous NPC fibre bootstrapping or campaign-wide adoption frequency.

## Render budget and limits

Original timber-frame/earth-infill home geometry reuses existing bounded materials and deterministic craftsmanship. Walls include real entrance/window gaps; foundation contacts use actual structural traces. No terrain edits, new lighting, blanket plinth or decorative free stored goods are added. Housing packet rendering is three shared instanced meshes, maximum 512 visible packets per material kind, drawn only from actual carried/dropped units. It does not invent an owned basket. These are source budgets, not measured GPU/FPS claims.

Absent housing fields leave existing saves and old huts unchanged. Terrain versions, generation/defaults and bank-render source are not changed by this patch. Ordinary new huts still use their original paid construction system. Full native wood harvesting/hut founding, broad multiplayer/campaign balance, natural NPC fibre supply and destroyed-project salvage recovery remain outside this bounded qualification.
