| Path | Current nominal work |
|---|---|
| Catch→cook fish | Catch ~1.4s/fish at dock or ~2.3s at bank; cook4/16s; ~5.4–6.3worker-seconds/nourishment. |
| Catch→smoke fish | Catch above; smoke4/30s; ~8.9–9.8worker-seconds/nourishment. |
| Catch→air dry fish | Catch above; work4/10s then60s passive exposure; ~3.9–4.8worker-seconds/nourishment, weather-sensitive. |
| Grain→porridge | Field labor varies; floor thresh8sheaves/12s, winnow6rough/8s→3.68grain+seed; porridge4grain/18s. Roughly10–11worker-seconds/nourishment with ordinaryfieldwork. |
| Grain→standard ovenbread | Above field/thresh/winnow; grind4/24s; bake4/24s;1.2nourishment/bread. Roughly15worker-seconds/nourishment. |
| Grain→bulk ovenbread | Grind16/48s and bake16/48s; roughly10worker-seconds/nourishment including ordinaryfieldwork. |
| Hunt→cook meat | Search/combat variable; butcher~2s/meat, cook4/20s;~7worker-seconds/nourishment plus hunting/travel. |
