# Independent annexation review: native transport and culture

The complete independent cohort passed **6/6** on the second frozen community-handover candidate: the prior four-case test plus two new native scenarios. Log: `/tmp/annexation-independent-final.log` (29.96 seconds). No runtime code was changed by this review.

A native V14 seed 1 coastal scenario paid 40 logs and four stone for an actual hull, boarded two real residents and sailed with twenty carried logs. A second hull was paid and advanced through real labor before being paused. Public annexation preserved the existing afloat hull, captain and passengers at their actual coordinates, the twenty logs, prior real water distance and second hull's paid progress. No practice was granted at ownership change. The paused state strictly saved before any tick; native terrain reconstructed identically. The same second hull then completed without another debit, while the original crew physically returned, disembarked and delivered the twenty original logs once. Its recipient practice increased on physical delivery only. The recipient's original nomadic primary home remained anchored.

A separate native V14 seed 345 scenario paid for real hearth firing of four initially declared dry vessels. After actual firing work, public annexation preserved progress and original village culture. The source hall appeared first in the building array; the recipient nevertheless kept its own primary hall. An immediate paused strict save restored, the same batch completed and actual vessels/shards were physically delivered under the new legal owner. At least one delivered vessel retained its original culture. No second material/fuel debit occurred.

These are finite authored ownership scenarios on unmodified native terrain. Existing buildings, starting bodies/goods and the transport fixture's initial night-work setting are declared endowment, not proof of an unmodified campaign. No stock, body, terrain or position repair was applied once each scenario began. The transport fixture retains an initially uninvolved society resident so an intentionally tiny world does not end before the return leg; the test rejects any simulation loop that stops advancing.

Keeper water/custody and inherited knowledge are separately owned test groups. Every mission/recipe/phase, long campaign balance and UI/performance remain outside this evidence.
