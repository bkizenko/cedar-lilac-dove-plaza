# Raw animal materials before the first hearth

Source delta against the frozen boat/diplomacy/frame integration cohort, isolated under .play/camp-processing-review. Source scope: production.ts, hunting.ts, types.ts, sim.ts, persistence.ts. No shared writes or staging. M1-10 remains pending broader joined review.

Before this repair, productionEnabled meant that a society currently had a completed hearth, drying rack or smokehouse. Hunting also used this facility predicate to decide whether animal kills had hide/bone potential and whether meat was raw. A new unbuilt camp therefore used the old direct edible-meat abstraction until its first station existed. Destroying its last station could restore that bypass.

Tribe.animalProcessingVersion is a strictly validated optional1. Every society in a genuinely fresh reset starts with1. animalProcessingEnabled controls animal raw-material rules; productionEnabled stays a pure facility-availability getter. A new camp's actual kill yields finite raw meat/hide/bone potential even before any building exists. Gutting, skinning, carrying and actual store delivery are still required. A real suitable workplace remains necessary for recipes; cooking still pays its fuel and labor. Raw meat starts spoiling before the first station, so the repair does not create free preservation.

Legacy absent flags preserve their previous abstraction until an actual own processing station is completed, or an already completed own station is loaded as migration evidence. Actual construction records the rule, and it persists after station destruction. Other societies do not gain it from a neighbor's station. Migration creates no structure or inventory. Already-carried edible meat is not reclassified: the existing rawHuntMeat marker still determines its delivery interpretation. Old dead carcasses are never backfilled with hides or bones. Mixed legacy worlds retain that compatibility limit: an old unupgraded hunter's previously unmodeled carcass byproducts are not invented when a fresh-rule collector later arrives.
