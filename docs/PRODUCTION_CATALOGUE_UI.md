# In-game crafting and workplace catalogue

`CraftingAndBuildings` provides a disclosure panel or an expanded ledger/dialog section. Root integration supplies the engine and optionally closes the surrounding dialog when a real structure site is chosen. It includes every actual `BUILD_ORDER` structure and25 typed workplace roles covering the earlier22 proposed chains plus preserved later stages. Planned production is explicitly labelled and has no invented unlock/build action.

Current structure entries derive their age, current society cost, material shortages, completed/unfinished counts and construction-plan state from the actual building registry and game state. Choosing a site uses the normal placement tool; it does not bypass age, foundation, materials, access or builder checks. Insufficient materials are visible because a drawing may wait unfunded.

First-cohort recipes are imported from `production.ts`, never duplicated in this catalogue: cooking meat, cleaning hides, extracting real bark tannin from a consumed log, drying skins, tanning leather, sewing skin garments and sewing leather garments. Exact inputs, logs/water, outputs, base hands-on seconds, finite slots and the actual `productionIssue` reason are shown for each matching completed workplace. Starting a batch calls the real production command. The view reports actual material inventory, item capacity, worn garments and paid projects. These controls do not grant inputs or immediately finish batches.

Distinct models in `productionWorkplaces.ts` represent the hearth ring/tripod, covered butcher bench, empty covered drying rack, tanning tubs/scraping frame and tailoring bench. Empty work surfaces do not show free meat/hides/clothes, and the hearth mesh has no eternal decorative fire. The normal building geometry registry must include the factory; physical job/cargo/state visuals remain a separate renderer qualification.

Food drying, pottery/clay extraction/firing, grinding/baking, fibre/weaving, charcoal/smelting/alloying, specialized hay/grain/well systems, cheese/curing, full repairs and staffed civic teaching remain explicitly incomplete. Existing buildings and foundations remain available; this catalogue does not redefine the historical era roadmap or add passed campaign gates. Source and focused tests alone do not qualify the full seasonal production campaign or desktop visual experience.
