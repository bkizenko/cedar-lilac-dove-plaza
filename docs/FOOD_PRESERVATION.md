# Loaded meat and fish preservation

Six physical recipes extend the existing processing executor. Four actual raw-meat items or four fresh-fish pantry units become four distinct dried/smoked edible units after loading, processing and delivery. Fish is removed from its real lot and the legacy food facade together; no new fish item or generic-provisions reward is created. Inputs are committed once. Existing finite carcass/hunting and fishing delivery paths remain the supply sources.

A completed drying rack has two shared slots for skin or food batches. Food needs10 hands-on loading seconds, then60 effective drying seconds determined by local temperature/humidity and weather. The loader is released during exposure, while the loaded batch retains its rack slot. Rain/storm/flood rewet it; cumulative120 wet seconds spoil the batch. Freezing pauses air drying, and initial wet/freezing weather gives a concrete start blocker. Pausing collection cannot pause weather or aging. When dry, the batch waits for an available adult with a real route, who walks to it and carries it to the cache. Direct movement orders, other paid tasks, guards and expeditions protect people from pickup reassignment. No carrier means no available stored food.

Manual hearth smoking requires60 hands-on seconds and two delivered logs per four food. The Stone-stage smokehouse costs12logs/6stone, requires ordinary paid physical construction, and has two staffed slots with30-second/one-log recipes. Its cover/fuelled method works during winter when air drying is frozen. Smoking fuel is separate from actual camp-heating fuel; both are debited from the same finite log stock and tracked. There is no passive smokehouse food or perpetual decorative flame.

The four pantry kinds are dried meat, dried fish, smoked meat and smoked fish. Their spoilage base rates are .000045/.000045/.000065/.000065 per simulation second, compared with cooked meat .0004 and fresh fish .00055, before the existing climate/storage/overflow modifiers. They have ordinary meal value, without multiplying physical output or altering the bread nourishment knob. A preserved batch held at its workplace/on its carrier expires after3600 seconds; fresh committed preservation inputs expire after360. These are explicit game-tuning rules, not scientific food-safety claims.

A drying project saves moisture, effective dry progress, elapsed/wet exposure, local temperature/humidity and pickup timing alongside actual workplace/body ownership. Curing/ready batches can be actorless; carrying/returning batches retain the physical carrier. Death before pickup leaves finished food at the workplace; death carrying it home loses the output. Workplace destruction loses loaded batches. Interrupted staffed work resumes its original paid progress and fuel. Strict validators reject impossible moisture/progress, forged ready states, malformed counters and missing ownership. Optional fields preserve older recipes/saves.

UI controls show actual fresh-fish inputs and named preserved outputs; queue bars show drying exposure rather than pretending the loading work is the complete recipe. The selected rack reports its occupied slots, weather, moisture and pickup blocker. Storehouse reports keep committed fish separate from available food and ready output separate from delivered stock. Catalogue food-drying/smoking roles are partial implemented purposes; salt, advanced chambers and detailed species-specific food safety remain planned. Existing skin drying remains its old staffed batch, explicitly distinct from food exposure.

Focused qualification uses controlled finite starting stores and actual Game.step travel/work/exposure/collection/delivery: six preservation scenarios cover saved passive drying, shared slot capacity, wet losses while paused, winter smoking with paid construction and a separate warmth-fuel ledger, missing-log rejection, saved staffed resumption, unavailable pickup, carrier loss, useful spoilage rates and corrupt-save rejection. The existing bread, hide/clothing, storage and catalogue tests remain regression gates. Natural-campaign winter economics, wind, detailed microbial safety, mixed-age food containers and browser visuals are separate qualification work. No campaign, graphical or scientific claim is inferred from these component scenarios.
