# Hearthwild user direction

- Desktop-only game and UI. Do not spend implementation or acceptance effort on mobile layouts, touch controls or mobile QA unless the user later requests it. This overrides the workspace's generic mobile guidance.
- Governing scope and order: docs/INCENTIVE_FIRST_MASTER_PLAN.md plus latest user corrections below. Keep the full campaign goal active; ship verified integrated batches and push regularly.
- Every generated map must contain the full requested geological feature catalogue, with visibly varied and explorable terrain. Do not substitute a high gentle-ground fraction, feature metadata, one smooth ridge or a few recolored slopes for real rivers, lakes, canyons, hills, mountain ranges, valleys, cliffs, terraces and plateaus, plus the requested pillar/waterfall/dune landscape families. Distribute across the map; every tiny island need not repeat everything. Vary prominence and composition, not whether a required family exists. Preserve safe usable starts and above-average measured player beauty. Research landform relationships and inspect rendered terrain at gameplay scale.
- Study the user's three October31 Dawn of Man images: rugged mountain basins, broad water, rolling/broken foreground, rocks and trees, usable fields and connected villages; strong stone/timber walls and towers. They guide functional original art, not copied assets.
- Conflict should emerge naturally from contextual orders and the situation. Do not force a pre-departure raid/conquest/domination picker. Looting, deliberate prisoner capture, intact occupation and tribute/domination remain distinct outcomes; allow plans to change in the field. Holding a settlement or accepting surrender must preserve usable buildings/residents, not silently burn them. Capture requires real custody/escort; domination requires sustained leverage and obligations, not just destruction.
- Latest Civ reference images require advanced surface treatment: material texture/roughness/normal variation, broken rock strata, layered ground cover, deep/shallow water, readable banks and shore foam, biome transitions and integrated dense settlements. Flat vertex colors alone are not the accepted finish. Preserve readable original game art and bounded desktop render cost.

- Every batch/push update must include percentages for the current batch and the full Classical-release acceptance checklist, with passed/total counts and the stable checklist version. These measure verified acceptance, not lines of code or an invented effort estimate. Keep partial/unverified items visible; do not silently change the denominator.
- Technology should be visible through a needs-driven knowledge/practice network: needs → observations/samples → experiments → demonstrated production → adoption/teaching. The player prioritizes practical problems and experiments; do not replace this with a Civ-style abstract science queue for named future inventions. Existing age thresholds remain until deliberately changed; expose actual blockers.
- Keep useful flats, but farms/crop rows follow sampled terrain and contours. Steep ground needs actual terrace work/access rather than automatic broad flattening; plot expansion must assess the full topography.
- Latest twelve context images inform observed first-contact settlement presentations, color illustrated earned historical moments, readable era/equipment-specific unit dress, biome/material building families and inhabited work yards, cliff/terraced settlements. Images are visual context, not permission to invent historical claims or import later-era machinery into early gameplay.
- Geography/biome checklist is docs/GEOGRAPHY_AND_BIOME_CATALOGUE.md. Every map must contain required major landform/water families and at least eight substantial ecological regions with functional/dressing differences; hills/valleys/rivers/lakes are never optional. Preserve full-area contour-aware farms. Additional scientific landform families are tracked explicitly without pretending current support.
- Latest map approval: the user's October31 12:37 archipelago screenshots show the desired complexity. Randomize island count, size and type; do not force every map to have many islands or every layout into one identical primary island. Every new map needs multiple substantial landmasses; one dominant showcase island plus tiny satellites does not satisfy the latest correction. Consider larger maps for spatially coherent feature/biome coverage. Preserve this positive reference alongside the earlier criticisms.
- New survival granularity: find actual wild animals before populating pens; distinguish species, pasture/grown feed, shepherd labor, maintenance/capacity. Pens need draggable size. Hunting uses actual owned weapons/projectiles and risk, followed by carcass transport/butchery, meat cooking/curing and hide drying/tanning; clothing differs for warmth/heat. Raw berries are easy but especially perishable without preservation. Water is actual collected inventory; hearth/fire consumes actual logs/fuel. Do not create free animal populations, cooked food, hides or duplicate fuel stock.
- Trenches must alter actual terrain as smooth traversable divots, with player-chosen depth, excavation work/volume, slopes and movement effects. A dark rectangular tile or raised trim does not count.
- Wealth/stock and accessible loot affect raid incentive; real weapons, fortifications, soldiers and successful defenses affect deterrence and loss estimates. Raiders can steal/capture and leave without annihilation, maintain real camps, and potentially transition into productive societies. Militia stand down after the threat and escort/occupation obligations end. Barracks must have real fortification behavior.
- Influence is a measured continuous field: full local influence extends to the old ring edge and fades beyond it. Mountains/water/route costs inhibit propagation; ships, ports, passes, roads and actual reach can change those costs. Further naval/port combat, siege, weapons and commander systems remain required design/implementation work, not delivered labels.
- Keep the normal24-hour clock and seasonal sunrise/sunset; advance clock minutes faster at night so daylight occupies88–94% of elapsed play time. Slow the clock cycle24x from the former default; keep simulation/work/consumption timers unchanged. Daytime must remain dominant even at4x. Review brightness and4x pacing. Player residents begin awaiting manual assignment; automatic work is an explicit available toggle. Direct orders remain reliable with automation off. Drinking-water collection remains automatic independently of general Auto work, using discovered reachable freshwater and actual free carriers. Different crops need distinct actual lifecycle visuals.

- Rival tribes must visibly send actual people exploring, build/crew/use real boats, raid and interact with each other as well as the player. All society pairs use shared physical/economic rules and their own observed information. Measure proposals, dispatches, arrivals, deliveries, conflict, losses and blockers per society and directed pair; no omniscient planner, fabricated boats or remote rewards.

- Multi-agent execution: use the available4 concurrent slots (integrator +3specialists) on independent ownership areas. Current priority includes an isolated urgent daylight fix while map/water/conflict work proceeds in parallel.
