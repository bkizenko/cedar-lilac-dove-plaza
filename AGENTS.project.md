# Hearthwild user direction

- Desktop-only game and UI. Do not spend implementation or acceptance effort on mobile layouts, touch controls or mobile QA unless the user later requests it. This overrides the workspace's generic mobile guidance.
- Governing scope and order: docs/INCENTIVE_FIRST_MASTER_PLAN.md plus latest user corrections below. Keep the full campaign goal active; ship verified integrated batches and push regularly.
- Every generated map must contain the full requested geological feature catalogue, with visibly varied and explorable terrain. Do not substitute a high gentle-ground fraction, feature metadata, one smooth ridge or a few recolored slopes for real rivers, lakes, canyons, hills, mountain ranges, valleys, cliffs, terraces and plateaus, plus the requested pillar/waterfall/dune landscape families. Distribute across the map; every tiny island need not repeat everything. Vary prominence and composition, not whether a required family exists. Preserve safe usable starts and above-average measured player beauty. Research landform relationships and inspect rendered terrain at gameplay scale.
- Study the user's three October31 Dawn of Man images: rugged mountain basins, broad water, rolling/broken foreground, rocks and trees, usable fields and connected villages; strong stone/timber walls and towers. They guide functional original art, not copied assets.
- Conflict should emerge naturally from contextual orders and the situation. Do not force a pre-departure raid/conquest/domination picker. Looting, deliberate prisoner capture, intact occupation and tribute/domination remain distinct outcomes; allow plans to change in the field. Holding a settlement or accepting surrender must preserve usable buildings/residents, not silently burn them. Capture requires real custody/escort; domination requires sustained leverage and obligations, not just destruction.
- Latest Civ reference images require advanced surface treatment: material texture/roughness/normal variation, broken rock strata, layered ground cover, deep/shallow water, readable banks and shore foam, biome transitions and integrated dense settlements. Flat vertex colors alone are not the accepted finish. Preserve readable original game art and bounded desktop render cost.
